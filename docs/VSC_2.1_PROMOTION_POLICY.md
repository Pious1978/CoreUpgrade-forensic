# VSC 2.1 — Research Promotion Policy

## Core Objectives
1. **Institutional Filtering:** Prevent noisy or unverified scanner outputs from entering the immutable governance chain.
2. **Multi-Factor Compliance:** Enforce explicit floors and ceilings across confidence, liquidity, and volatility metrics.
3. **Audit Traceability:** Every promotion decision (Promoted or Rejected) must carry explicit rationale and metrics for audit logs.
4. **Baseline Invariance:** The policy layer sits strictly inside the `research/` boundary, leaving downstream VSC contracts and orchestrators untouched.
