SCRIPT TRACKER - CoreUpgrade-forensic Root Pipeline
=====================================================
Plain-English notes on what each real, active script does - matching
the same style as your own Alpha1/Obsolete/Script Tracker.txt, so the
same kind of at-a-glance reference exists for this system too.

Covers the real, verified, currently-active pipeline only. Does not
cover the separate Canonical OMS Spine architecture (oms/, risk/,
control_plane/, contracts/ packages, and the build_gate*/certify.py/
wire_contracts.py files) - that's a real, separate, already-proven
system with its own history, not something this tracker speaks to.
Also does not cover Scrap/ (confirmed-dead code) or the diagnostic
check_*.py scripts written purely as one-off verification tools.


DATA LAYER
----------

bhav_to_parquet_converter.py - Converts the daily NSE bhav copy CSV
into the parquet_cache your whole system runs on. Preserves any
Yahoo-backfilled older history rather than overwriting it - only
regenerates the date range the new bhav copy actually covers.

backfill_from_yahoo_archive.py - The one-time (already-run) tool that
extended ~2,050 stocks from ~38 days of real history to 1.5-2 years,
by splicing in older Yahoo Finance data - but only after verifying it
genuinely agrees with real bhav-copy prices on overlapping dates, so a
hidden stock split or dividend adjustment can't quietly corrupt the
data. 8 real splits were correctly caught and rejected this way.

Universe_Updater.py - Intentionally NOT part of the automated pipeline.
Its live NSE fetch proved unreliable (a partial response once caused it
to treat 268 actively-trading stocks as delisted and delete real data).
Keep this manual until it has a sanity check against implausibly large
single-day universe changes.


MARKET INTELLIGENCE
--------------------

RelativeStrengthEngine.py - Computes each stock's relative-strength
percentile (how it's performed vs. the market, ranked against the
FULL universe, not a pre-filtered shortlist - ranking against a small
pool inflates every score) and a delivery-percentage score. Writes to
daily_snapshot. Requires 252+ real trading days of history before
including a stock, specifically to keep newly-listed IPOs from
distorting the ranking for everyone else.

Market_Regime_Engine.py - Figures out whether the overall market is in
a genuine uptrend, a recovery, distribution, a real bear phase, or just
choppy - using real market breadth (% of the whole universe trading
above their own 20/50/200-day averages) plus the Nifty's own trend.
This used to be a library-only file with no persistence layer at all -
nothing ever wrote its output to the database, so every regime-aware
calculation in the system was silently running on a hardcoded NEUTRAL
fallback. Now genuinely computes and saves a real regime every run.


DISCOVERY ENGINES (run conditionally based on the real regime above)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Consolidation_Scanner.py, Hybrid_Alpha_Scanner.py,
Emerging_Leader_Scanner.py, Earnings_Gap_Scanner.py, Cup_and_Handle.py
- The five bullish, continuation-pattern scanners. Look for stocks
building a tight base and getting ready to break out. Run when the
regime is anything except BEAR or DISTRIBUTION.

Bear_Market_Scanner.py - Fundamentally different philosophy: looks for
stocks that have genuinely fallen 25-80% from their 52-week high but
show real signs of stabilizing - oversold RSI, holding a meaningful
Fibonacci retracement level, and critically, genuine relative strength
against the index despite the fall (an anti-value-trap filter - a
stock that's fallen AND is still underperforming the market gets
rejected, not just any stock that's fallen a lot). Runs instead of the
five bullish scanners when the regime is BEAR or DISTRIBUTION.


RANKING AND CONSENSUS
-----------------------

Confirmation_Factor_Generator.py - Computes live-relevant confirmation
factors (intraday/weekly RVOL, pivot extension) for candidates that
cleared the discovery engines.

core/factor_registry.py - The shared definition of which factors belong
to which weighted family (relative_strength, institutional, structure,
confirmation, risk_liquidity) - the single source of truth several
other scripts pull their weighting scheme from.

Pivot_Consensus_Engine.py - Where multiple scanners might flag the same
stock with different suggested pivot levels; resolves that down to one
real, agreed pivot per stock.

Master_Terminal.py - Pulls everything together into one ranked research
watchlist, with a Composite_Score and Tier. The tier assignment now
also cross-checks real relative-strength percentile (ranked against the
whole universe, a much larger and more stable pool) before allowing the
top tier - stops small-candidate-pool score inflation alone from
promoting a stock that isn't genuinely strong.


RISK AND EXECUTION PLANNING
-----------------------------

Risk_Positioning_Engine.py - Turns each research candidate into an
actual, sized trade plan: real 14-day ATR-based stop-loss, a 20%
per-stock concentration cap, and dynamic profit targets that adjust
based on the real current market regime and the setup's own quality
score - rather than the same fixed 2:1/3:1 reward-to-risk on every
single trade regardless of context.

Execution_Plan_Generator.py - Downstream formatting/output of the risk
layer's sized plans.


LIVE MONITORING AND TOOLS
----------------------------

Live_Execution_Monitor.py - The main live terminal you actually watch
during the day. Tracks every candidate's real-time state (approaching,
testing, valid breakout, stop breached, etc.), a real multi-factor
conviction score split into Opportunity and Readiness, extension-based
sizing rejection, candle-body fakeout detection, RVOL-aware messaging
that distinguishes a heavy-volume stop breach from a possible shakeout,
Expected-Days-to-Pivot estimates, how long a stock has genuinely been
sitting in its current state (persists across sessions), a 3-step exit
plan that tracks whether Target 1 has actually been reached, and a real
Position Monitor section for whatever you've told Trade_Journal.py
you're actually holding - using your real entry price, not a
hypothetical one.

Stock_Lookup.py - Ad-hoc, on-demand analysis for any single ticker,
whether or not it made the overnight scan - the "something just moved,
check it right now" tool. Falls back to a genuine 20-day-high pivot
estimate if the ticker was never flagged by any scanner. Includes a
volatility-adjusted position-sizing factor and a PULLBACK_ENTRY tag
(price below EMA20 in an established EMA20>EMA50 uptrend) as a
complementary read alongside the main pivot-based state. Prints
guidance on when its read is actually trustworthy during the day
(avoid the first 15 minutes after open - RVOL is noise before real
volume builds up).

Trade_Journal.py - Where you log what you actually did: sync today's
alerts, log a real execution, log a full or partial exit (tracked as
separate, real exit events so a partial profit at T1 doesn't stay
invisible to your win-rate statistics until the rest of the position
eventually closes too), see open positions, see real statistics, or
log a manual/past entry for anything that was never generated by this
system's own alerts.


ORCHESTRATION
--------------

Pipeline_DAG_Executor.py - Runs the whole nightly sequence in order,
with retries and checkpoints. Reads the real, current regime right
after Market_Regime_Engine.py runs, and picks the bullish discovery
engines or Bear_Market_Scanner.py accordingly before continuing on to
ranking, consensus, risk, and execution planning.
